# What if the world could only eat Olive Garden? — Beat sheet (Step A)

**Format:** same as the 2008 video. 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · 62.5 s (13 beats × 4.5 s + a 4 s end card).
**Always on screen:**
- the title, "What if the world could only eat Olive Garden?"
- a caption with a REAL fact
- a stats panel with one **REAL** row and one **WHAT IF** row
- a small "Parody · not affiliated with Olive Garden or Darden" tag

**Look:** the same toy-like miniature city, now with a food-court Main Street: a taco truck, a sushi bar, a burger joint, a pizza place, a noodle shop and a bakery. The restaurants are drawn as generic Tuscan-style buildings with a plain-text sign. There is no copy of the real logo.
**Sound:** an original "trattoria" loop written in code: mandolin plucks with tremolo, a wheezy accordion and an upright-bass bounce, plus a pop or boing for every joke.

## The what-if assumption

**One change:** starting tomorrow, every restaurant, grocery store and kitchen on Earth can only serve the Olive Garden menu.

**Everything else stays the same:** people, prices, laws and the real company as it exists today.

**How imagined outcomes are shown:** in words only, never as numbers presented as facts. The one exception is clearly labeled **napkin math**: plain multiplication of two real numbers, such as calories per breadstick × breadsticks.

**Producer notes:**
- **Weight gain:** the "people getting fatter" idea is played through objects: belt buckles popping off, a stretchy-pants shop selling out, breadstick baskets that never empty. The little people never change shape. That keeps the joke on the food, not on bodies, and avoids the body-shaming angle TikTok tends to suppress.
- **No claims about the real company:** the video never says Olive Garden makes anyone unhealthy. It only does calorie arithmetic with the published breadstick number.

## Notes for review

- **Napkin math in beats 6 and 10** is plain multiplication of real numbers, labeled "napkin math", not presented as a prediction.
- **Beat 7: the 2015 sale is the one-second sellout.** The commonly repeated "sold out in 1 second" story is about **2015** (2,000 passes). The first pass in 2014 took about 45 minutes, so the caption says 2015.
- **Beat 1 and beat 2 counts:** 935 is Olive Garden's **company-owned US count** at the end of FY2025. Darden's total across all its brands was 2,159.
- **No real logo or branding.** The company names appear only as plain text in captions and signs, and the parody tag stays on screen the whole time.
