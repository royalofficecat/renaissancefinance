# What if the world could only eat Olive Garden? — Beat sheet (approved, with notes applied)

**Format:** 1080 × 1920 vertical · 30 fps · 62.5 s (13 beats × 4.5 s + a 4 s end card).
**Always on screen:** the title, a caption with a REAL fact, a stats panel (one **REAL** or **NAPKIN MATH** row + one **WHAT IF** row), a "WHAT IF CITY · imagined" badge, and a "Parody · not affiliated with Olive Garden or Darden" line.
**Look:** the shared toy-city toolkit with a Tuscan food-court Main Street. No real logo: company names appear only as plain text.
**Sound:** an original trattoria loop written in code (tremolo mandolin, accordion, upright bass, tambourine) plus a pop or boing for every joke. The band plays softer on the Italy twist.

## The what-if assumption
Starting tomorrow, every restaurant and kitchen on Earth can only serve the Olive Garden menu. Everything else stays the same.
- **REAL** = a sourced fact with a number.
- **NAPKIN MATH** = plain arithmetic on two real numbers, shown for fun, not as a prediction.
- **WHAT IF** = imagined, always in words.
- "Getting fatter" is played through objects (belt buckles, the stretchy-pants shop, never-empty baskets). The little people never change shape.

## Napkin-math assumptions
- Beat 6: 14 × 140 = 1,960 calories.
- Beat 9: $1 trillion ÷ $12.1 billion ≈ 83; Walmart's $681B is the comparison.
- Beat 10: 8.2 billion × about 6 inches (15 cm) ≈ 1.2 million km a day; the Moon round trip is about 769,000 km, so one day's breadsticks reach the Moon and back with room to spare.
- Beat 11: $500 ÷ $13.99 ≈ 35.7, so it pays off on bowl 36.
