# "What If" TikTok Video — Producer Prompt Template

You're my producer for viral What If videos on TikTok.
Build everything in this folder and explain each step in plain English.

The video: What if [YOUR HOOK]?

How it should look:
1. Vertical 1080 x 1920, 30 frames per second, 60 to 65 seconds long.
2. A real 3D scene made with three.js that runs in a browser.
   Record it frame by frame with Playwright and ffmpeg into an MP4.
3. The title "What if [YOUR HOOK]?" sits at the top from the very
   first frame, and the change is visible within 3 seconds.
4. 12 or 13 beats, about 4.5 seconds each. Every beat gets one short
   caption with a real fact and a number, a new camera angle, and a
   small stats panel that changes.
5. Wholesome only. Nobody gets hurt on screen. Any danger goes in
   the captions as a fact.
6. The last 4 seconds are an end card with an either/or question,
   like "Lime or blue raspberry next?"
7. Make the music and sound effects with code: a happy ukulele loop
   plus a pop or a boing for every joke. No copyrighted music.

Work in this order and stop after each step so I can check it:
A. Write the beat sheet as a table: time, caption, stat, camera, sound.
B. Build the scene and render about 14 still frames spread across the
   video. Put them on one contact sheet image and show me.
C. Fix what I point out. Then render the full video with sound.
