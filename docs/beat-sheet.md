# Beat sheet — "What if California got 30 days of extreme rain?"

64 seconds total = 60 s of story (13 beats, 4 or 6 s each) + 4 s end card. 1,920 frames at 30 fps.
Music runs at 120 bpm, so one bar is exactly 2.0 s (60 frames) and every beat change lands on the first beat of a bar.

The hook title is on screen from frame 1 and stays for beat 1. A day counter (italic, top left, "Day 1" to "Day 30") runs through the storm beats so the time scale is always visible. Every flood layer on a map carries the label *Illustrative scenario*.

Caption rules applied: one idea each, two lines, 38 characters per line max, key number in bold, no full stop. Reading time: at 3 words per second a caption needs at most one third of its slot for a first read, so 4 s beats carry 12 words or fewer, 3 s slots 9 or fewer, 6 s beats 18 or fewer. (Checked by script; the longest is 12 words in a 4 s beat, and the 10-word closing line gets a 3.5 s slot.)

## Arc and visual resets

Hook (1) → escalate (2–5) → surprise and go bigger (6–7, the lake) → consequences (8–9) → turn (10–11) → payoff (12–13) → end card.
Resets every 3–4 beats: beat 4 (jump from map to a soil cube), beat 6 (top-down map, color shifts bluer), beat 10 (rain stops, palette warms toward green).

## Sound cue sheet

Music: 120 bpm, D major. Bars 1–8 (beats 1–3): rain bed, soft pad and plucked tones. Bars 9–20 (beats 4–9): pulse enters at bar 7, locks in with full drums at bar 11 (beat 6), minor-leaning chords (B minor) from bar 13, peak at bars 14–16 (beat 7). Bars 21–24 (beats 10–11): drums thin to a heartbeat, piano enters, back to D major. Bars 25–30 (beats 12–13): piano lead with warm pad, payoff hit on bar 25. Bars 31–32 (end card): button note, fade.
Effects (all code-generated): rain bed (filtered noise, intensity per beat), map whoosh (beats 2, 6, 8), count-up ticks (every stat panel, one tick per 0.3 s of count-up), water swell (3, 7), gate whoosh (11), electrical drop (9), payoff boom (12), button (end). Music sits 9 dB under effects; master to −14 LUFS, peaks under −1 dB.
