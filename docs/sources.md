**Changed from the brief, on the evidence:**
- **Atmospheric rivers:** the brief said "roughly 40–60% of California's annual precipitation". NOAA's consistent figure is **30–50%** for the West Coast states, and the peer-reviewed California-specific estimate is 20–50% (Dettinger et al. 2011). The video uses NOAA's 30–50%.
- **Valley length:** the often-quoted 450 miles has no USGS or DWR source; USGS says **about 400 miles**.
- **Lake Oroville:** DWR re-surveyed it in 2024 (3.42 million acre-feet, down from 3.54), so the video leads with Shasta Lake, whose figure is stable.
- **1861–62 duration:** USGS says 45 days and other sources say 43, so the caption says "about 45 days".
