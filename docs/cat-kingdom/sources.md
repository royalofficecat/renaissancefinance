## Map data (the real base layer)

| Layer | Used for | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| California outline, state borders, coastline (1:10m) | Every map | [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) via [natural-earth-vector](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector) | Public domain |
| Cities: Sacramento, San Francisco, San Jose, Los Angeles, Santa Ana / Irvine, Palm Springs, Fresno | City dots and labels | Natural Earth populated places | Public domain |
| Urban areas (city footprints) | Where the miniature cities sit on the map | Natural Earth 1:10m urban areas | Public domain |
| Major roads and freeways | The road network the human service routes follow | Natural Earth 1:10m roads | Public domain |
| Elevation | Mountains (2× relief), Central Valley floor | [Terrain Tiles on AWS](https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/), from USGS 3DEP / SRTM / NOAA ETOPO1 / INEGI | Open data with attribution (below) |

**Elevation attribution:** United States 3DEP (formerly NED) and SRTM terrain data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey · Global ETOPO1 terrain data, U.S. National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration · Mexico terrain data source: INEGI, Continental relief, 2016 · tiles by Mapzen.

**Invented map layers (fictional, marked so in the legend):** feline territories, rooftop routes, sun estates, wildlife sanctuaries and the human service network. They are drawn over real geography but represent nothing real.
